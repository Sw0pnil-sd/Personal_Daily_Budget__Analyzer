## Project Abstract
The Personal Finance and Daily Budget/Spend Analyzer is designed to help users better understand and manage their monthly finances. Users will be able to import transaction data, enter their monthly income and fixed expenses, and view categorized spending summaries. The system will calculate total spending, remaining disposable income, and a recommended daily spending amount based on the money available and the number of days left in the month. As additional expenses are recorded, the daily budget will update automatically to help users stay within their financial limits and make better day-to-day spending decisions.
